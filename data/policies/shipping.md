# Shipping Policy

*Sample policy for demo purposes only. Sunleaf Tea Co. is a fictional business.*

## Where we ship

We ship to India, the United States, the United Kingdom and countries in the European Union. We do not ship to other countries at the moment.

## Dispatch and delivery times

Orders are dispatched within 2 working days. Delivery inside India usually takes 3 to 6 working days. International delivery usually takes 7 to 14 working days after dispatch.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more inside India. Below ₹999, a flat fee of ₹79 applies. International shipping is calculated at checkout from the parcel weight and destination.

## Customs and duties

International orders may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country. These are paid by the customer.

## Tracking

A tracking link is sent by email as soon as the order is dispatched.
