# Privacy Policy

*Sample policy for demo purposes only. Sunleaf Tea Co. is a fictional business.*

## What we collect

We collect your name, email address, delivery address and order history so we can process and deliver your orders.

## How we use it

We use your details only to fulfil orders, send order updates and, if you opt in, our newsletter. We never sell your personal data.

## Payments

Card payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not store card numbers.

## Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time by writing to support@example.com. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter from any email we send.
