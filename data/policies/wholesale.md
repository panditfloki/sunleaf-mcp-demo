# Wholesale Policy

*Sample policy for demo purposes only. Sunleaf Tea Co. is a fictional business.*

## Who can apply

Cafes, restaurants, hotels and retailers in India can apply for a wholesale account. Wholesale orders ship inside India only. We review applications within 5 working days.

## Minimum orders

The first wholesale order must be at least 5 kg of tea across any blends. Repeat orders have a 2 kg minimum.

## Pricing and payment

Wholesale prices are 30% below retail for orders up to 20 kg and 40% below retail above 20 kg. Payment is due within 15 days of invoice.

## Custom blends

Custom blends are available to partners ordering 20 kg or more per blend. Development takes about 4 weeks, including two rounds of samples.
