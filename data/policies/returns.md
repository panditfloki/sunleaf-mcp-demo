# Returns Policy

*Sample policy for demo purposes only. Sunleaf Tea Co. is a fictional business.*

## What can be returned

Unopened tea and unused teaware can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, in their original packaging. Opened tea cannot be returned unless it arrived damaged or faulty (see Opened tins below).

## Opened tins

For food safety reasons, opened tea cannot be returned. If a tin arrives damaged, or the tea inside is faulty, tell us within 7 days of delivery and include a photo. We will replace it or refund you.

## Damaged or broken items

If teaware arrives broken, send a photo within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement at no cost, and you do not need to send the broken item back.

## Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we receive and check the returned item.

## Gift cards

Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash.
